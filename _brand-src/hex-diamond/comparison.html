AI Foundry logo: directions

The same construction four ways. The hexagon is the Builder Hexagon (flat top and bottom, as the app draws it); the inner shape is the Consultant Diamond. A, B and C use a square diamond and differ in the weight of the hexagon, the weight of the diamond, and the clear space between them. D turns the diamond into a rhombus whose sides run parallel to the hexagon's slanted sides, so every line in the symbol sits at 0 or 60 degrees. Type is the brand nameplate: Archivo 700 at width 125, outlined.
