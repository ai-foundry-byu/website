AI Foundry logo: directions

The same construction nine ways. The hexagon is the Builder Hexagon (flat top and bottom, as the app draws it); the inner shape is the Consultant Diamond. A, B and C use an outlined diamond and differ in the weight of the hexagon, the weight of the diamond, and the clear space between them. D turns the diamond into a rhombus whose sides run parallel to the hexagon's slanted sides, so every line in the symbol sits at 0 or 60 degrees. E drops the inner outline: the hexagon is solid and the diamond is cut out of it as the O's counter, so there is one shape and nothing to balance. F cuts a true square diamond out of the hexagon, nearly as large as it can go: its top and bottom points stop a hairline band short of the flats, so the hexagon stays whole, and its side corners stop short of the hexagon's points. G stretches the diamond so all four corners touch, which leaves four triangles but makes it wider than tall. H inverts the roles: the diamond is the letter and the hexagon is cut out of it as the counter. I reverses H's fills: a solid hexagon inside a diamond outline. In every direction but D, the diamond is proportioned so that, scaled up from the center, its four corners reach the hexagon together: top and bottom at the flats, left and right at the points. Type is the brand nameplate: Archivo 700 at width 125, outlined.
