# AI Foundry brand narrative

Approved by Scott Murff, 2026-11-17. Use verbatim; each section is a bold heading on its own line.

**Who we are.**
An AI-native product studio and consultancy at the BYU Marriott School of Business, and an experiential learning program of BYU Strategy. Students build working AI software for real organizations.

**Why we exist.**
To develop disciple-leaders who use AI to enhance human flourishing. We share BYU Marriott's mission, to develop leaders of faith, intellect, and character, and its vision, to transform the world through Christlike leadership.

**What we do.**
We train AI builder consultants. A builder can make things for themselves. A builder consultant makes something for someone else, which adds judgment, ownership of the outcome, communication, and collaboration to the work. Every student grows in both: the six skills of the Builder Profile and the four of the Consultant Profile.

**What our mark means.**
The O of FOUNDRY is a hexagon with a diamond at its centre. The hexagon is the builder, one cell of the beehive Brigham Young adopted as a symbol of industry and shared work. The diamond is the consultant and leader. Like a diamond reflects light, we seek to reflect the Light of Christ.
